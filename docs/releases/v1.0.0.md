# SagaBurst v1.0.0

The first public release of SagaBurst brings Viking and Roman battles to your browser and desktop.

## Play

- **Browser:** [Play Online](https://andy-ch-bo-an.github.io/SagaBurst-3D-Game/) on a desktop computer with keyboard and mouse.
- **Windows x64:** download `SagaBurst-v1.0.0-Windows-x64.zip`, extract the entire folder, and open `SagaBurst.exe`.
- **macOS Apple Silicon:** download `SagaBurst-v1.0.0-macOS-arm64.zip`, extract it, and open `SagaBurst.app` (preferably from Applications).

No Node.js, npm, Git or terminal is needed. Desktop builds include the game assets and work offline.

## What's playable

- Custom Viking vs Roman battles with up to 200 AI troops per side, infantry, ranged units and cavalry. Fight in person, command your army, or spectate.
- Roman and Viking Defense Campaigns, each with nine stages and separate saved progression.
- Career Mode: explore a fortified faction town, earn merit and promotions, complete missions, and purchase equipment and mounts.
- Personal Squads and the HR Center for officers, plus Town Defense and Enemy Town Assault missions.
- Mounted combat and Black Cat / Corgi free rides.

## First launch and saves

These v1 builds are unsigned and are not notarized. macOS may require right-click → Open; if blocked, try opening once, then go to System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway. Windows may show Microsoft Defender SmartScreen; use More info → Run anyway if you trust this GitHub download.

Career and Campaign saves stay on the device. Browser saves and desktop saves are separate and do not sync. Clearing site data or deleting the app's user data removes saves. Click the game view to capture the mouse; press Esc to release it.
