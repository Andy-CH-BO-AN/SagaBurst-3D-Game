# SagaBurst v1.2.0

Take to the skies on xongkoro, practice in the new Training Ground, and lead larger forces through the expanded Captain career.

## Play / 開始遊玩

- **Browser / 瀏覽器：** [Play Online](https://andy-ch-bo-an.github.io/SagaBurst-3D-Game/) with a keyboard and mouse.
- **Windows x64:** extract `SagaBurst-v1.2.0-Windows-x64.zip` and open `SagaBurst.exe`.
- **macOS Apple Silicon:** extract `SagaBurst-v1.2.0-macOS-arm64.zip`, move `SagaBurst.app` to Applications, and follow the included `MACOS-FIRST-LAUNCH.txt`.

Desktop downloads include all game assets and work offline. See the player guides in [English](https://github.com/Andy-CH-BO-AN/SagaBurst-3D-Game/blob/v1.2.0/README.md) or [繁體中文](https://github.com/Andy-CH-BO-AN/SagaBurst-3D-Game/blob/v1.2.0/README.zh-TW.md) for controls and modes.

## Highlights

- **Training Ground:** freely try weapons, shields, and every mount; test damage on 0–100 m dummies, practice mounted charges, refill supplies, and reset the scene. Includes normal-warrior and T4 Ranger practice without Career XP.
- **xongkoro giant eagle:** fly, bank, accelerate, land, attack with the eagle, and aim ranged weapons from its back. Career Captains and Commanders can purchase the mount; town eagle defenders also join the fighting.
- **Six Captain missions:** Patrol Command, Cavalry Command, Gate Defense, Frontline Commander, Siege Commander, and a 30-vs-30 Eagle Battle with additional Personal Squad support.
- **More command opportunities:** Captain and Commander characters receive a permanent 30-person Town Command Squad alongside their separately recruited Personal Squad. Mission command authority and command merit follow the troops assigned to the player.
- **T4 equipment:** Roman Paladin appearance and shared T4 weapons and shield, a T4 Sergeant, and the Ranger Bow in Career shops.
- **Town improvements:** clearer shop and military districts, larger eagle training areas, queued eagle arrivals, and more precise landing at assigned pads.
- **Combat and squad fixes:** improved mounted NPC interaction and remounting, first-person eagle aiming, allied-mount projectile protection, squad HUD counts, mission equipment restrictions, and troop return/refit behavior between missions.
- **Updated player guides:** English and Traditional Chinese READMEs explain current modes, progression, controls, commands, and saves.

## 本版重點

- **訓練場：**免費試用武器、盾牌與所有坐騎，測試 0–100 公尺假人傷害、練習騎乘衝撞、補給與重置；可切換一般戰士或 T4 遊俠，不累積 Career XP。
- **xongkoro 巨鷹：**新增飛行、轉彎、加速、降落、鷹擊與鷹背遠程瞄準。Career 的 Captain／Commander 可購買，城鎮巨鷹守軍也會投入戰鬥。
- **六種 Captain 任務：**巡邏指揮、騎兵清剿、城門防衛、前線指揮、攻城與 30 對 30 巨鷹空戰；私人小隊可額外參戰。
- **更多部隊指揮：**Captain／Commander 取得常駐 30 人城鎮指揮小隊，與自己招募的私人小隊分開；任務授權與指揮軍功依玩家實際指揮的部隊計算。
- **T4 裝備：**Roman Paladin 外觀、共用 T4 武器與盾牌、T4 士官長，以及 Career 商店的遊俠弓。
- **城鎮更新：**商店與軍事區重新配置、巨鷹訓練區擴大、降落排隊與指定停機位的降落更精準。
- **戰鬥與小隊修正：**改善騎乘 NPC 互動、重新上馬、巨鷹第一人稱瞄準、友軍坐騎投射物誤傷、小隊 HUD 人數、任務換裝提示，以及任務間返營和整補流程。
- **玩家指南：**中英文 README 更新目前玩法、成長、操作、命令與存檔說明。

## Saves and first launch / 存檔與首次開啟

Existing Career and Campaign saves remain on the device, with support for the revised town layout. Browser and desktop saves are separate and do not sync. Replacing the desktop app preserves its existing user data.

既有 Career 與 Campaign 存檔仍留在裝置上，並支援更新後的城鎮配置。瀏覽器與桌面版存檔各自保存、不會同步；替換桌面程式會保留既有使用者資料。

The macOS app is ad-hoc signed but not Apple-notarized; follow the included first-launch guide if macOS blocks it. Windows is unsigned and may display SmartScreen; for the official download, use **More info → Run anyway** if you trust it.

macOS 版有 ad-hoc 簽章，尚未經 Apple 公證；若被阻擋，請依隨附的首次開啟說明操作。Windows 版未做程式簽署，可能顯示 SmartScreen；確認信任官方下載後，可選「其他資訊 → 仍要執行」。
