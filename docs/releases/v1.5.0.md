# SagaBurst v1.5.0

Lead T5 commanders, defend the bridgehead and all four town gates, and explore rebuilt towns with walkable ramparts, mounted races, and expanded army management.

## Play / 開始遊玩

- **Browser / 瀏覽器：** [Play Online](https://andy-ch-bo-an.github.io/SagaBurst-3D-Game/) with a keyboard and mouse.
- **Windows x64:** extract `SagaBurst-v1.5.0-Windows-x64.zip` and open `SagaBurst.exe`.
- **macOS Apple Silicon:** extract `SagaBurst-v1.5.0-macOS-arm64.zip`, move `SagaBurst.app` to Applications, and follow the included `MACOS-FIRST-LAUNCH.txt`.

Desktop downloads include all game assets and work offline. See the player guides in [English](https://github.com/Andy-CH-BO-AN/SagaBurst-3D-Game/blob/v1.5.0/README.md) or [繁體中文](https://github.com/Andy-CH-BO-AN/SagaBurst-3D-Game/blob/v1.5.0/README.zh-TW.md) for controls, progression, and saves.

## Highlights

- **Commander career:** five Commander missions, including aerial raids, a T5 commander assault, Outpost Last Stand, Bridgehead Battle, and Four-Gate Town Defense. Bridgehead Battle pits 200 infantry against 300 cavalry after three minutes of preparation; Four-Gate Town Defense brings 230 attackers and eight siege ramps, with a 15-minute defense objective.
- **T5 progression and army management:** T5 commanders, duels, recruitment, weapons, and player equipment; command eight town ground squads, organize civilians, buy equipment and mounts in bulk, refit squads, and sell unused town equipment.
- **Walkable fortifications:** rebuilt town and outpost ramparts, stairs, gatehouse bridges, an elevated command watchtower, autonomous archers, mobile siege ramps, and breach assaults.
- **Mounted races and retirement:** six ground-mount races with saved results and personal bests. Eligible Commanders can apply for retirement after ten Commander mission victories, including a Four-Gate Town Defense win.
- **Updated characters and mounts:** new faction infantry, civilians, T4 rangers, T5 commanders, and bandits; lighter animated horses, black cats, and corgis, with restored horse coat variety and improved mounted weapon poses.
- **Combat and recovery fixes:** reversible NPC ranged/melee switching, restored gate attacks, safer siege and eagle movement, improved town navigation, clearer squad counts, and more reliable mission returns and resident recovery.
- **Performance and player guides:** reduced character, mount, projectile, navigation, siege, and checkpoint overhead; refreshed English and Traditional Chinese guides explain current gameplay without revealing the retirement outcome.

## 本版重點

- **Commander 生涯：**五項指揮官任務，包含空襲、T5 斬首、前哨決戰、橋頭決戰與四門總攻守城戰。橋頭決戰有三分鐘準備，以 200 名步兵迎擊 300 名騎兵；四門守城迎戰 230 名攻軍與八台攻城坡道車，目標守住 15 分鐘。
- **T5 成長與部隊管理：**新增 T5 指揮官、單挑、招募、武器與玩家裝備；可指揮八支城鎮地面小隊、編組平民、批次購買裝備和坐騎、整補小隊，以及出售閒置城鎮裝備。
- **可行走的城防設施：**重建城鎮與前哨站城牆步道、階梯和城門橋，加入可騎乘登上的指揮瞭望塔、自主弓兵、移動攻城坡道車與破門進攻。
- **坐騎競賽與退休：**六場地面坐騎繞城競賽，保存成績與個人最佳紀錄。累積十場 Commander 任務勝利，且至少贏過一次四門守城戰後，可申請退休。
- **人物與坐騎更新：**更新陣營步兵、平民、T4 遊俠、T5 指揮官與盜匪；換用較輕量的戰馬、黑貓和柯基動畫模型，恢復馬匹毛色變化並改善騎乘武器姿勢。
- **戰鬥與恢復修正：**NPC 能依戰況切換遠近戰，修復城門攻擊、攻城與巨鷹移動、城鎮尋路、小隊人數，以及任務返城和居民恢復。
- **效能與玩家指南：**降低人物、坐騎、投射物、尋路、攻城與存檔檢查點的運算負擔；中英文指南更新目前玩法，並保留退休結局的探索空間。

## Saves and first launch / 存檔與首次開啟

Existing Career and Campaign saves remain on the device; updated town layouts include migration and recovery handling. Browser and desktop saves are separate and do not sync. Replacing the desktop app preserves its existing user data.

既有 Career 與 Campaign 存檔保留在裝置上，城鎮配置更新包含遷移與恢復處理。瀏覽器與桌面版存檔各自保存、不會同步；替換桌面程式會保留既有使用者資料。

The macOS app is ad-hoc signed but not Apple-notarized; follow the included first-launch guide if macOS blocks it. Windows is unsigned and may display SmartScreen; for the official download, use **More info → Run anyway** if you trust it.

macOS 版有 ad-hoc 簽章，尚未經 Apple 公證；若被阻擋，請依隨附的首次開啟說明操作。Windows 版未做程式簽署，可能顯示 SmartScreen；確認信任官方下載後，可選「其他資訊 → 仍要執行」。
