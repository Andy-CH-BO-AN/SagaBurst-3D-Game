# SagaBurst v2.1.0

Play Career with a friend over LAN co-op, defend the Commander V town against four gates at full strength, skip the bridgehead and gate preparation when you are ready, and enjoy a round of fixes across Career, mounts and NPC combat.

## Play / 開始遊玩

- **Browser / 瀏覽器：** [Play Online](https://andy-ch-bo-an.github.io/SagaBurst-3D-Game/) with a keyboard and mouse.
- **Windows x64:** extract `SagaBurst-v2.1.0-Windows-x64.zip` and open `SagaBurst.exe`.
- **macOS Apple Silicon:** extract `SagaBurst-v2.1.0-macOS-arm64.zip`, move `SagaBurst.app` to Applications, and follow the included `MACOS-FIRST-LAUNCH.txt`.

Desktop downloads include all game assets and work offline. See the player guides in [English](https://github.com/Andy-CH-BO-AN/SagaBurst-3D-Game/blob/v2.1.0/README.md) or [繁體中文](https://github.com/Andy-CH-BO-AN/SagaBurst-3D-Game/blob/v2.1.0/README.zh-TW.md) for controls, progression, and saves.

## Highlights

- **LAN co-op (new):** choose **LAN Co-op** on the main menu with a friend on the same WiFi or LAN. The host creates a room (the desktop app opens it automatically on port 47820) and the guest enters the host's IP address to join. Together you enter Career battlefields, the Career Town, arena duels (2v1), mounted races (three riders, the fastest earns the reward), and the prison break. The host commands the official mission troops as usual; the guest commands its own HR personal squad and fights with its own character, hero, equipment and mount from its own save.
- **Four-Gate Town Defense, intensified:** a fresh four-gate attack starts at full strength, with no carried-over Town HP, casualties, spent mounts or squad wounds. Enemy lancers are released to charge 150 m from their gate, attacker archers deploy ahead of their vehicles beside the lane, and the Town's gate garrison grows to 20 T3 soldiers per gate who stand on the wall and can be commanded through their gate's infantry squad. Civilians stay inside the Town Center hall.
- **Skip preparation:** in Bridgehead and Four-Gate Defense, press `H` to end the 3-minute deployment countdown early. Co-op guests cannot skip; the HUD shows the hint during preparation.
- **Co-op fixes:** the guest follows the host's Town return, sees siege vehicles, fights Town enemies, rides stable horses, earns four-gate merit, and the host commands the mission's official troops again.
- **Career and mounts:** Veteran outpost battles resume from the main-menu Career entry; a Captain caught mid-return at the North Gate walks home correctly after a reload; merchants can ride every Town mount and equip the best T5 shield; cavalry no longer circle waypoints, and horse archers no longer stall.

## 本版重點

- **區網合作（新功能）：**主選單選擇「區網合作」，與在同一個 WiFi／區網的朋友一起出戰。主機建立房間（桌面版會自動在連接埠 47820 開房），隊友輸入主機的 IP 加入。雙方可一起進入生涯戰場、生涯城鎮、競技場單挑（2v1）、賽馬（三人同場，最快者獲得獎勵）與越獄戰。主機照常指揮任務的正式部隊；隊友指揮自己的 HR 私兵，並以自己存檔中的角色、英雄、裝備與坐騎參戰。
- **四門守城戰強化：**新的四門攻勢以滿編狀態開始，不帶入城鎮血量、戰損、已消耗的坐騎或私兵傷勢。敵方槍騎會在距城門 150 公尺處才脫離守備、發動衝鋒；攻方弓兵部署在攻城車前方的車道兩側；城內守軍增加到每門 20 名 T3 士兵，站上城牆，並編入該門步兵小隊，玩家可直接指揮。平民會留在城鎮中心大廳內。
- **跳過準備時間：**在前哨決戰與四門守城戰中，按 `H` 可提前結束 3 分鐘部署倒數；合作隊友不能跳過，準備期間 HUD 會顯示提示。
- **合作修正：**隊友會跟著主機返回城鎮，看得到攻城車、會與城鎮敵軍交戰、能騎乘駐馬，並可獲得四門守城的軍功；主機重新能指揮任務的正式部隊。
- **生涯與坐騎修正：**從主選單的生涯入口可接續老兵前哨戰；任務中途重新載入時，在北門的隊長會正確走回城內；商人可騎乘全部城鎮坐騎並裝備最佳 T5 盾牌；騎兵不再繞著路徑點打轉，騎弓兵也不會卡住。

## Saves and first launch / 存檔與首次開啟

Career (`sagaburst_career_v2`) and Defense Campaign (`sagaburst_defense_campaign_progress_v1`) save formats are unchanged; existing saves remain on the device. LAN co-op does not change save formats: a guest plays on an in-memory copy of the host's scene and keeps only the skill progress and race rewards it earns in its own save. Browser and desktop saves are separate and do not sync. Replacing the desktop app preserves its existing user data.

既有的 Career（`sagaburst_career_v2`）與防守戰役（`sagaburst_defense_campaign_progress_v1`）存檔格式不變，舊存檔仍留在裝置上。區網合作不改變存檔格式：隊友在主機場景的記憶體副本中遊玩，只把自己獲得的技能進度與賽馬獎勵保存到自己的存檔。瀏覽器與桌面版存檔各自保存、不會同步；替換桌面程式會保留既有使用者資料。

The macOS app is ad-hoc signed but not Apple-notarized; follow the included first-launch guide if macOS blocks it. Windows is unsigned and may display SmartScreen; for the official download, use **More info → Run anyway** if you trust it.

macOS 版有 ad-hoc 簽章，尚未經 Apple 公證；若被阻擋，請依隨附的首次開啟說明操作。Windows 版未做程式簽署，可能顯示 SmartScreen；確認信任官方下載後，可選「其他資訊 → 仍要執行」。
