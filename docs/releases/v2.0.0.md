# SagaBurst v2.0.0

Choose between two classes at Captain and Commander, fight in the Roman Colosseum and the Viking Valhalla, break out of prison after a lost battle, and lead heavy cavalry, feline legions, and 300-vs-300 custom battles in rebuilt Roman and Viking towns.

## Play / 開始遊玩

- **Browser / 瀏覽器：** [Play Online](https://andy-ch-bo-an.github.io/SagaBurst-3D-Game/) with a keyboard and mouse.
- **Windows x64:** extract `SagaBurst-v2.0.0-Windows-x64.zip` and open `SagaBurst.exe`.
- **macOS Apple Silicon:** extract `SagaBurst-v2.0.0-macOS-arm64.zip`, move `SagaBurst.app` to Applications, and follow the included `MACOS-FIRST-LAUNCH.txt`.

Desktop downloads include all game assets and work offline. See the player guides in [English](https://github.com/Andy-CH-BO-AN/SagaBurst-3D-Game/blob/v2.0.0/README.md) or [繁體中文](https://github.com/Andy-CH-BO-AN/SagaBurst-3D-Game/blob/v2.0.0/README.zh-TW.md) for controls, progression, and saves.

## Important: new Career save / 重要：Career 新存檔

v2.0.0 uses a new Career save format. **Career characters from v1.x are not loaded; start a new Career.** Old data is left on the device and is not converted or deleted. Defense Campaign stage progress is kept; Custom Battle and Campaign setup screens start from their defaults once.

v2.0.0 改用新的 Career 存檔格式，**v1.x 的 Career 角色不會載入，請建立新角色。**舊資料仍留在裝置上，不會被轉換或刪除。防守戰役的關卡進度會保留；自訂戰鬥與防守戰役的設定畫面會先回到預設值一次。

## Highlights

- **Two class roads per faction:** Roman Praetorian Captain or Centurion, Viking Varangian Captain or Dragon Oathguard at T4; Roman Legate or Byzantine Cataphract, Norse Warchief or Ironblood Warlord at T5. Heavy classes hit harder and take less damage but move more slowly. Buy the second class later and switch freely; recruit either class into your Personal Squad.
- **Arenas:** new Roman Colosseum and Viking Valhalla venues. Duels add unarmed fights, T4 / T5 opponent class selection, and in-arena `Tab` equipment. New **Arena Squad Trials** (T1–T5) pit you and your own-culture team against another squad without permanent losses.
- **Heavy cavalry missions and prison break:** Iron Encounter, Iron Tide, Steel Avalanche, and Commander VII Iron Legion, with allied lancers on the wings. Lose one of the iron battles and fight your way out of the captor's arena with your fellow prisoners.
- **Feline legion and mounts:** Commander VI Six Feline Cavalry, Orange/White Cat, Serval, Caracal and Armored Horse mounts, mount armor, continuous cavalry charges, mount stat comparison in shops, and six sprint circuit races.
- **New skills:** Unarmed and **Armor Break** (part of blocked damage passes through enemy shields and mount armor) join a unified seven-skill panel.
- **Custom Battle 2.0:** up to 300 vs 300 on the ground or 50 vs 50 eagle archers in the air, ten squads per side, T1–T5 for every unit type, and five maps including the Roman and Viking towns.
- **Rebuilt towns:** expanded Roman and Viking town centers and civic buildings, gatehouses joined to the walls, indoor service NPCs, integrated eagle facilities, and story-driven milestone guidance that points you to your next step.
- **Simpler mission board:** obsolete early missions are retired and missions no longer unlock in a chain; your rank opens each tier and mounted missions only need a mount you own.
- **Controls and command:** hold `F` to aim or raise a shield (trackpad friendly), and cyan markers show which soldiers receive your order.
- **Characters and performance:** new tiered faction soldiers, refreshed T4 / T5 heroes, bandits and weapons; KTX2 textures and many large-battle CPU and rendering optimizations for smoother 300-vs-300 fights.

## 本版重點

- **雙職業路線：**T4 羅馬可選禁衛軍隊長或百夫長、維京可選瓦良格隊長或龍誓重衛；T5 羅馬可選羅馬軍團統帥或拜占庭鐵甲重騎兵、維京可選北境戰酋或鐵血戰帥。重甲職業攻擊更強、受傷更低但移動較慢；之後可購買第二職業並自由切換，私兵也能招募兩種職業。
- **競技場：**新增羅馬競技場與維京英靈殿。單挑加入徒手對決、T4／T5 對手職業選擇與場內 `Tab` 換裝；新增 **競技場軍團試煉**（T1–T5），與同文化隊友組隊對戰，不會造成永久傷亡。
- **重騎兵任務與越獄：**遭遇鐵騎、鐵騎破陣、鋼鐵雪崩與 Commander VII 鐵甲軍團決戰，並有友軍槍騎兵分列兩翼。在鐵騎戰役敗北後，要和其他戰俘一起從俘虜方競技場殺出重圍。
- **貓騎軍團與坐騎：**Commander VI 六貓騎兵團，新增橘貓／白貓、藪貓、獰貓與重甲戰馬；坐騎護甲、騎兵連續衝鋒、商店坐騎能力比較，以及六場全程衝刺的繞城賽馬。
- **新技能：**徒手格鬥與**破甲**（被敵方盾牌與坐騎重甲擋下的傷害部分穿透），技能面板統一顯示七項技能。
- **自訂戰鬥 2.0：**地面最多 300 對 300、空中最多 50 對 50 巨鷹弓手，每方十個小隊，所有兵種皆可配置 T1–T5，並有包含羅馬與維京城鎮在內的五張地圖。
- **城鎮重建：**羅馬與維京城鎮中心與公共建築擴建、城門樓接入城牆、服務 NPC 移入室內、巨鷹設施整合，並有依進度觸發的城鎮劇情指引。
- **任務板簡化：**淘汰過時的早期任務，任務不再依序解鎖；軍階決定可接階級，騎乘任務只需要自有坐騎。
- **操作與指揮：**按住 `F` 可瞄準或舉盾（方便觸控板），命令模式會以青色標記顯示收到命令的士兵。
- **人物與效能：**新增分階陣營士兵，更新 T4／T5 英雄、盜匪與武器；改用 KTX2 貼圖並大量降低大型戰鬥的 CPU 與繪製成本，300 對 300 更流暢。

## Saves and first launch / 存檔與首次開啟

Browser and desktop saves are separate and do not sync. Replacing the desktop app preserves its existing user data.

瀏覽器與桌面版存檔各自保存、不會同步；替換桌面程式會保留既有使用者資料。

The macOS app is ad-hoc signed but not Apple-notarized; follow the included first-launch guide if macOS blocks it. Windows is unsigned and may display SmartScreen; for the official download, use **More info → Run anyway** if you trust it.

macOS 版有 ad-hoc 簽章，尚未經 Apple 公證；若被阻擋，請依隨附的首次開啟說明操作。Windows 版未做程式簽署，可能顯示 SmartScreen；確認信任官方下載後，可選「其他資訊 → 仍要執行」。
